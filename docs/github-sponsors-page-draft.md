## Monthly tiers

### $5 a month

**Supporter.** Help support Preflight and my open-source work. Your sponsorship contributes to
development, testing, release work, hosting, and the other costs that come with keeping projects
maintained.

### $10 a month

**Backer.** Give a little more toward ongoing development, compatibility work, testing, and future
projects. A good fit if you use Preflight regularly or like following what I make.

### $20 a month

**Sustainer.** Help cover a meaningful share of the ongoing work behind Preflight: compatibility
testing, release preparation, cross-platform testing, hardware, hosting, and maintenance.

### $50 a month

**Sponsor.** For people, modders, developers, or organizations who want to make a substantial
contribution to Preflight and my other work.

## One-time tier

### $10 one time

**One-time supporter.** If Preflight helped you out and you want to throw a few bucks toward the
work, thank you. One-time support goes toward the same development and testing costs as monthly
sponsorships.
